# Quick recipe: Full (more going on)

Same base as Just you with more layered on. Busy but readable: never more than one extra thing on screen at once.
**Use for:** stories, hot takes, anything with big reactions.
**Needs:** one talking-head take, filmed vertical. Length 25-50 s.

## Look (sizes on a 1080x1920 frame)
| | Spec |
|---|---|
| Hook | Brand hook (brand font, default Inter ExtraBold, 64 px, brand ink on white boxes), top at y ~270 |
| Captions | Brand font (default Inter ExtraBold) 56 px, white on a brand-ink box, 1-3 words, y ~1540, **one keyword per line in the brand accent** |
| Caption motion | Word pop (90% -> 100%); keyword pops a touch bigger (105%) |
| Palette | Brand ink (default `#141414`), brand accent (default `#FF5A36`), white, plus a yellow `#FFD84D` sticker accent allowed for GIF frames |
| Cards | As Just you, plus a card on every list item; alternate left / right of her head |
| GIFs | ~28% frame width, upper corner, never over her face, in for 1.5-2.5 s, one every ~8-10 s |
| Zooms | 112% punch-in on every second beat, 125% on the 2-3 biggest lines; hard cut in, no slow push |
| Grade | +5 contrast, +5 saturation. No filters |
| Pacing | 1.3x, a visual change every 2-4 s (cut, zoom, card or GIF) |
| Sound | One per moment from the SKILL.md moment map, about -16 dB. Music optional, low (you add it in the Edits window) |

## Hook formulas
- "Nobody tells you this about [thing] 😬"
- "The worst [client/money/tech] mistake I made this year"
- "I said yes to [thing] and here's what happened"

## Presets to save in Edits
- Text: **Brand hook** (PNG). Captions: **Brand caption pop** (brand caption + accent highlight on the key word).

## Recipe
1. Cut + 1.3x as Just you, a little tighter.
2. Hook as Just you.
3. Captions brand caption pop; choose the keyword per line (the word she stresses).
4. Cards on every named thing and list item; GIFs on funny or emotional beats; zooms on big lines.
5. Count extras at every second: never two at once.

## Do / don't
- Do line every sound up with the thing it belongs to.
- Don't stack a GIF, a card and a zoom on the same beat. Don't use more than one pink word per line.
