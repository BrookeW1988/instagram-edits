# Quick recipe: Text over B-roll ("text it like a friend")

**Looks like:** 5-10 s of everyday footage (coffee pickup, walking, the desk, the shopfront) with one casual line
of text, written the way you'd text a friend. No talking. Familiar song over trending audio.
Micro-lesson variant: one tip taught in 2-4 short lines over B-roll in 8-12 s.
**Reference:** [Krys Benyamein reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZz6ubJxH0i/); micro-lesson variant:
[Duolingo reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcJaVCfRGZi/) (BAM 27/06/2026, Async 21/08/2026).
**Use for:** camera-shy members, busy days, relatable one-liners. Effort: low.
**Needs:** 1-3 short B-roll clips (no face needed) + the line. If she has no line, offer 3 in her voice.

## Look (sizes on a 1080x1920 frame)
| | Spec |
|---|---|
| Text font | Instrument Sans Medium, 46 px, lowercase ([Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Sans)) |
| Text treatment | White, no box, soft shadow (0 2 12 px, black 45%). Busy footage only: white pill at 85% with ink text |
| Layout | Max 2 lines, ~24 characters per line, left-aligned, x 90, y ~720 (upper-middle, away from the UI at the bottom) |
| Motion | Simple fade/pop in at 0.3 s; holds to the end. Micro-lesson: each line appears on a beat, previous lines stay |
| Palette | White text only. The footage provides the colour |
| Grade | Warm film: +warmth, lifted blacks, -contrast, a little grain if Edits has it; same grade on every clip |
| Footage | Steady, slow movement, natural light; hands and objects rather than faces |
| Pacing | 5-10 s total (micro-lesson 8-12 s), 1-3 clips, normal or slightly slowed speed |
| Sound | No SFX. Music: a familiar song the member adds from Edits' library in the app (tell her the mood) |

## Text formulas (lowercase, no full stop)
- "me pretending inbox zero is a personality trait"
- "the client email that made me raise my prices"
- "pov: you finally automated the boring bit"

## Presets to save in Edits
- Text: **Friend text** (Instrument Sans Medium, white, shadow).

## Recipe
1. Trim clips to the steadiest bits; total 5-10 s; same grade on all.
2. One text line in Friend text; hold long enough to read twice.
3. Make the end flow back into the start (it should loop cleanly).

## Do / don't
- Do write like a text message: lowercase, no jargon, no hashtags on screen.
- Don't add captions, stickers or sound effects. Don't place text where Instagram's buttons sit (bottom 20%, right edge).
