# Quick recipe: Split-screen "Yes... but"

**Looks like:** the top half labelled YES shows the dream; the bottom half labelled BUT shows the catch. Text labels
only, trending audio. Self-aware and funny. **Reference:** Instagram example pending (SocialBee latest trends,
07/10/2026).
**Use for:** "the reality of running a business", "what clients think vs what actually happens". Effort: low-medium.
**Needs:** two clips (the dream, the reality) + two short labels. Offer label ideas if she has none. Length 5-12 s.

## Look (sizes on a 1080x1920 frame)
| | Spec |
|---|---|
| Label font | Anton, 72 px, ALL CAPS ("YES:" / "BUT:") ([Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Anton)) |
| Label treatment | White, 6 px black outline (meme style), top-left of each half (x 50, y 60 within the half) |
| Sub-label | Inter Bold 40 px, white, 4 px outline, under the label ("laptop on the beach" / "47 unread emails") |
| Layout | Edits split screen, top and bottom halves, each clip cropped to fill its half; 6 px white divider |
| Palette | White, black |
| Grade | **Top (YES):** bright, warm, +saturation. **Bottom (BUT):** cooler, -saturation, slightly dim |
| Timing | Both play together the whole way; or the bottom appears 0.5-1 s after the top for comic timing |
| Sound | No SFX (or one "record scratch" as BUT appears). Music: trending audio added by the member in the app |

## Label formulas
- "YES: I work from anywhere" / "BUT: anywhere has bad wifi"
- "YES: I'm my own boss" / "BUT: my boss never logs off"
- "YES: the client loved it" / "BUT: 'can we try one more version'"

## Presets to save in Edits
- Text: **Yes label**, **But label**, **Split sub-label**.

## Recipe
1. Crop both clips to fill their half (no letterboxing, no stretched faces).
2. Labels + sub-labels; grade the halves differently.
3. Decide timing: together, or BUT lands a beat later.

## Do / don't
- Do keep labels short enough to read in a second each.
- Don't add captions. Don't let the two halves have the same grade (the contrast is the joke).
