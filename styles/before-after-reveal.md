# Quick recipe: Before-and-after / "the process" reveal

**Looks like:** opens on the worst state with a question ("Would you grab this from the trash?"), quick one-second
process cuts, then holds on the result about two seconds longer than feels comfortable. Often ends on a
comment-keyword CTA. **Reference:** [Erin Shuford reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcO1EWSgTDG/)
(Async 21/08/2026; Later "The process" 18/09/2026).
**Use for:** product brands, stylists, organisers, designers, renovators. Effort: medium.
**Needs:** a "before" clip, several short process clips, an "after" clip. Length 12-30 s.

## Look (sizes on a 1080x1920 frame)
| | Spec |
|---|---|
| Labels | Bebas Neue, 96 px, CAPS ("BEFORE", "AFTER"), white on a black plate at 80%, top-left x 60, y 160 ([Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Bebas+Neue)) |
| Question hook | Inter Bold 58 px, white on ink `#111111` pill, centred y ~320, first 2 s |
| Captions | Only with speech or voiceover: Inter Bold 48 px, white, 6 px outline, y ~1450 |
| Palette | Black, white, gold `#F5C451` (the AFTER label plate and one sparkle sticker) |
| Grade | **Before:** cooler, -15 saturation, slightly flat. **After:** warm, +10 saturation, +contrast. Grade them separately |
| Process | Clips 0.5-1 s each, cut on movement, long actions sped up 2-4x |
| Reveal | Quick white flash (3-4 frames) or a fast 100% -> 110% push into the after shot, then hold 3-4 s |
| Sound | Tactile sound per key action (`searchSoundEffects`: the action itself, e.g. "sanding", "scissors", "tape"), one "sparkle"/"ding" on the reveal. Music: member adds in app |

## Hook formulas
- "Would you [keep/buy/grab] this?"
- "[Price/time] later..." / "Watch what [N] hours does"
- "My client said it couldn't be saved"

## Presets to save in Edits
- Text: **Before label**, **After label** (gold plate), **Reveal question**.

## Recipe
1. 0-2 s: before shot, cool grade, BEFORE label + question hook.
2. Process montage, in order, sped up; a few tactile sounds.
3. Reveal: flash or push, AFTER label in gold, warm grade, hold 3-4 s with one sparkle sound.
4. CTA keyword only if the member already has that funnel. Never invent one.

## Do / don't
- Do make the difference obvious in one glance (same angle for before and after if possible).
- Don't cut the reveal hold short. Don't put music-only beats where a tactile sound would sell the work.
