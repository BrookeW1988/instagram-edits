# Quick recipe: POV micro-skit

**Looks like:** on-screen "POV: the client finally sends feedback..." while the creator acts it out or does an
everyday task, with one reaction beat and no outro.
**Reference (format only; the account promotes an app):**
[hannah.shapes reel](https://www.instagram.com/hannah.shapes/reel/DcHZeQNy76e/) (Async 21/08/2026, PodcastVideos 11/09/2026).
**Use for:** playful personalities; service providers acting out client moments people recognise. Effort: low.
**Needs:** one short acted clip (5-15 s) + the POV line. If she has none, offer 3 in her voice.

## Look (sizes on a 1080x1920 frame)
| | Spec |
|---|---|
| Text font | Poppins SemiBold, 48 px, sentence case ([Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Poppins)) |
| Text treatment | Ink `#1A1A1A` on a white pill, radius 14, padding 12/22 (the native Instagram look), max 2 lines |
| Layout | Centred, top third, y ~300; up from frame 1 to the end |
| Reaction beat | Hard punch-in to 130% on her face for the last 1-1.5 s, plus one sound or one 3D emoji (Microsoft Fluent Emoji 3D, free, MIT licence, or the free editing pack) |
| Captions | Only if she speaks: Poppins SemiBold 44 px, white, black outline, y ~1450 |
| Palette | White pill, ink text, the emoji provides the colour |
| Grade | Natural, phone-camera look (that's the point) |
| Pacing | 5-15 s total; setup -> reaction; no outro |
| Sound | One reaction sound (`searchSoundEffects`: "record scratch", "crickets", "gasp", "boing") or none. Music: trending audio added by the member in the app |

## Text formulas
- "POV: the client finally sends feedback..."
- "POV: you said 'quick question' and it wasn't"
- "POV: you open your books after 3 months"

## Presets to save in Edits
- Text: **POV text** (Poppins SemiBold, white pill).

## Recipe
1. Trim to setup + reaction; cut on the reaction, nothing after.
2. POV text from frame 1.
3. Punch-in + one sound or emoji on the reaction.

## Do / don't
- Do make the POV line readable in under 2 s.
- Don't add a hook on top of the POV line. Don't explain the joke in captions.
