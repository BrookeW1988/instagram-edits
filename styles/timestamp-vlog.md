# Quick recipe: Timestamp vlog / appointment timestamps

**Looks like:** each clip labelled with a time ("9:02 am client arrives ... 10:15 the reveal"), swipe transitions,
no voiceover, upbeat track. **Reference:** Instagram example pending (Later Reels trends: "Vlog timestamps"
23/10/2026, "Appointment timestamps" 13/11/2026).
**Use for:** service businesses with a visible process (hair, beauty, clinics, organisers, photographers) and
"day in my business". Effort: low.
**Needs:** 5-10 short clips through the day + the time for each. If she doesn't know the times, use each clip's
recorded time (file metadata) and say so. Length 10-25 s.

## Look (sizes on a 1080x1920 frame)
| | Spec |
|---|---|
| Timestamp font | JetBrains Mono Medium, 40 px, lowercase am/pm ([Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/JetBrains+Mono)) |
| Timestamp chip | White text on black at 60%, radius 10, padding 8/16, format "9:02 am · client arrives", top-left x 60, y 140 |
| Opening title | Optional: Instrument Serif Italic 72 px "a day in my business" centred, first 1.5 s ([Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Serif)) |
| Transitions | One swipe-left (~6 frames) between every clip, the same one throughout |
| Palette | White, black chip; the footage provides colour |
| Grade | Soft, bright, warm "daylight": +exposure, -contrast, +warmth; one look on every clip |
| Pacing | Clips 1-3 s, final reveal/result 3-4 s |
| Sound | Optional soft "whoosh" on 2-3 swipes; a "camera shutter" on the final result. Music: upbeat track added by the member in the app |

## Label formulas
- "6:45 am · coffee before chaos"
- "9:02 am · client arrives"
- "4:30 pm · the reveal"

## Presets to save in Edits
- Text: **Timestamp** (chip), **Day title** (serif italic).

## Recipe
1. Clips in time order; trim to 1-3 s each.
2. Timestamp chip on every clip, exact same spot and style.
3. Same swipe on every cut; final shot held 3-4 s.

## Do / don't
- Do keep times going forwards.
- Don't add captions or voiceover. Don't mix transition types.
