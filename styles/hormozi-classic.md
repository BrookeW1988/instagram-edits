# Quick recipe: Hormozi classic (fast-cut bold captions)

**Looks like:** big bold all-caps captions, white with a black outline, the spoken word lit up; a cut every 1-3 s,
punch-in zooms, one plain background, one framing, no music.
**Reference:** [Alex Hormozi](https://www.instagram.com/hormozi/) (caption-tool breakdowns; highlight colour varies
by source: yellow or green).
**Use for:** direct, high-energy teaching: business, money, mindset. Effort: low.
**Needs:** one talking-head take against a plain background, filmed vertical. Length 20-45 s.

## Look (sizes on a 1080x1920 frame)
| | Spec |
|---|---|
| Caption font | Montserrat Black, 84 px, ALL CAPS, tracking +1% ([Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Montserrat)) |
| Caption treatment | White fill, 10 px black outline, soft drop shadow; no box |
| Active word | Highlight yellow `#FFE600` (or green `#3CFF6E`, or the member's accent), same font |
| Caption layout | 2-3 words per line, max 2 lines, centred at y ~1150 (just under the chin) |
| Caption motion | Each word pops (110% -> 100%) as it's spoken |
| Hook | Optional: the first caption line IS the hook; or one Montserrat Black 72 px line at y ~300 for 2 s |
| Zooms | Alternate 100% / 118% framing every 2-3 cuts; 130% on the single biggest line. Hard cuts, no slow pushes |
| Palette | White, black, one highlight colour. Nothing else |
| Grade | Punchy: +10 contrast, -5 saturation, slightly cool. Plain dark or neutral background |
| Pacing | 1.2-1.3x; a cut or zoom change every 1-3 s; zero dead air |
| Sound | None, or one whoosh on the 130% zoom. No music |

## Hook formulas
- "STOP [common mistake]" / "YOU'RE UNDERCHARGING"
- "[Number] THINGS I'D DO IF I STARTED AGAIN"
- "THE REASON YOUR [thing] ISN'T WORKING"

## Presets to save in Edits
- Captions: **Fast captions** (per-word highlight, Montserrat Black caps, outline).

## Recipe
1. Cut every pause and restart; keep words whole (no clipped endings).
2. Captions with per-word highlight in the Fast captions preset.
3. Zoom changes on cuts, never mid-word.

## Do / don't
- Do proofread every caption: in this style the words are the visuals.
- Don't add GIFs, cards or music. Don't use more than one highlight colour.
