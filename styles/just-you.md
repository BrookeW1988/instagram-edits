# Quick recipe: Just you (raw, less edited)

Inspired by Taki Wong's (@taki.gpt) boxed-hook reels. Mostly your face and captions; the edit stays out of the way.
**Use for:** tool lists, updates, how-tos where things get named one by one. Default when nobody picks.
**Needs:** one talking-head take, filmed vertical. Length 20-45 s.

## Look (sizes on a 1080x1920 frame)
| | Spec |
|---|---|
| Hook font | Bold SF Pro (on every Mac: `/System/Library/Fonts/SFNS.ttf`), ~72-76 px (auto-fit), sentence case. Inter ExtraBold if SF Pro isn't there |
| Hook box | White rounded box per line, lines TOUCHING (no gap), one hard offset shadow so the white reads on any ground, centred, top at y ~270-300, never over her face. Logo badge (real logo + tool name) above the box when the reel is about one tool |
| Captions | Brand font (profile.md; default Inter Bold) 54 px, white on a brand-ink box (radius 12), 1-3 words, y ~1540 (low on the chest) |
| Caption motion | Pop in (90% -> 100%), no word highlight |
| Palette | Brand ink (profile.md; default `#141414`) for text and caption box, white (hook boxes, card frames), brand accent (default `#FF5A36`, one accent only), brand cream (default `#FAF7F2`) |
| Cards | Real screenshot/logo, white frame 8 px, radius 24, soft shadow, ~38% frame width, above her head |
| Grade | Natural. Fix HDR first. No filters. Slight warmth only if the room is cold |
| Pacing | 1.3x. Cuts only at pauses and restarts (every 3-6 s). No jump-cut-for-energy |
| Sound | Soft whoosh or pop under a card landing (`searchSoundEffects`: "soft whoosh", "pop"), about -18 dB under her voice. No music |

## Hook formulas
- "I tried [N] [tools] this week and [one] blew my mind 🤯"
- "[Tool] just added [thing] and nobody's talking about it 🤯"
- "Stop doing [task] by hand. Do this 👇"

## Presets to save in Edits
- Text: **Brand hook** (drawn as a PNG, see SKILL.md). Captions: **Brand caption**.

## Recipe
1. Cut pauses and restarts, keep complete thoughts, 1.3x (or her usual speed from profile.md).
2. Hook: brand hook, 2 lines (3 max), 5-9 words, one emoji at the end (🤯 default, never 🚀). Held ~3-6 s through
   the spoken hook, then gone.
3. Captions: brand caption, off during the hook, proofread every word.
4. Cards: one small card only when she names a real thing, in on the word, out when she moves on.
5. GIFs: none, or one in the whole reel.

## Do / don't
- Do let whole stretches be just her and captions.
- Don't add zooms, music, emoji stickers or a second accent colour. If it feels busy, take things out.
