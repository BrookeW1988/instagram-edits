# Quick recipe: Pop-up proof

**Looks like:** hook pinned at the top, a sub-hook types on underneath, then at ~2.5-3 s a real proof screenshot
pops up (DM, client result, review, follower graph). Tight cuts; lines cut from the audio come back as text.
**Reference:** [Shannon McKinstrie](https://www.instagram.com/shannonmckinstrie/) (Social Media Examiner,
24/09/2026, "Why Unpolished Reels Win").
**Use for:** coaches and service providers with results to show. Effort: medium.
**Needs:** one talking-head take + 1-4 real proof screenshots. Ask the member for HER screenshots first. Never invent, mock up or borrow anyone else's proof.
Blur other people's names and faces unless they agreed. Length 20-40 s.

## Look (sizes on a 1080x1920 frame)
| | Spec |
|---|---|
| Hook font | Inter ExtraBold, 62 px, sentence case ([Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter)) |
| Hook box | Ink `#111111` text on a white pill, radius 999 (fully round ends), padding 14/28, top at y ~220 |
| Sub-hook | DM Mono Medium 36 px ([Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Mono)), white on ink at 75% opacity, radius 10, typewriter entrance (~0.8 s), y ~330 |
| Captions | Inter Bold 50 px, white with a 6 px black outline, no box, 2-4 words, y ~1450 |
| Proof card | Screenshot in a white frame 10 px, radius 28, shadow, ~80% frame width, upper half (y 420-1000), tilted -2° |
| Callout | A hand-drawn circle or arrow (your own file, or a callout from the free editing pack) on the key number, in yellow `#FFE45C` |
| Palette | Ink `#111111`, white, highlighter yellow `#FFE45C` (callouts only) |
| Grade | Bright and clean: +5 exposure if the room is dim, natural colour |
| Pacing | 1.1-1.3x, every pause out, cut every 2-4 s |
| Sound | Typing under the sub-hook ("keyboard typing", <2 s), a pop when each proof lands ("pop", "paper"), ~-18 dB. No music |

## Hook formulas
- "How I got [result] without [the usual thing]" + sub-hook "(here's the proof)"
- "My client went from [before] to [after] in [time]" + sub-hook "(screenshot below)"
- "Everyone said [myth]. Then this happened"

## Presets to save in Edits
- Text: **Proof hook** (white pill) and **Proof subhook** (mono, typewriter). Captions: **Proof caption** (outline, no box).

## Recipe
1. Tight cut, every pause out.
2. Hook pinned 0-3 s; sub-hook types on at ~0.5 s with the typing sound.
3. First proof lands at ~2.5-3 s with a pop; hold ~2 s per line of text on it. Callout circle on the key number.
4. Captions on, but hide them while a proof card is up if they clash.
5. A strong line cut for time comes back as a short text pop-up in the hook style.

## Do / don't
- Do make every proof readable on a phone (crop tight to the important bit).
- Don't cover her face with proof. Don't use fake or "example" screenshots.
