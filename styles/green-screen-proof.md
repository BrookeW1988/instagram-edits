# Quick recipe: Green-screen "proof behind me"

**Looks like:** the creator talks in front of the screenshot, article, comment or chart she's reacting to. The image
is the proof, she's the commentary. Captions on, music low or off.
**Reference:** Instagram example pending (Virlo green-screen data, 06/10-16/11/2026; examples found were TikTok).
**Use for:** industry news, answering a comment, myth-busting. Effort: low.
**Needs:** a talking-head take + the image(s). Real sources only; blur private names. Length 20-45 s.

## Look (sizes on a 1080x1920 frame)
| | Spec |
|---|---|
| Hook font | Space Grotesk Bold, 60 px ([Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Grotesk)) |
| Hook treatment | Black text on a highlighter-yellow `#FFF34F` marker stripe (square ends, slight -1° tilt), y ~200 |
| Captions | Space Grotesk Bold 52 px, white on black box radius 6, 2-3 words, y ~1600 |
| Image | Fills the top 55-65% of the frame, white 6 px frame + shadow; zoomed so the key line is big |
| Source chip | Space Mono 24 px ([Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Mono)), "Source: [site]", white on black 70%, under the image |
| Person | Subject cut-out, bottom 40-45% of frame, centred |
| Markup | Red `#E63946` hand-drawn circle or underline (your own file, or a callout from the free editing pack) on the key line |
| Palette | Newsprint `#F4F1EA` (behind the image), black, highlighter yellow `#FFF34F`, red `#E63946` |
| Grade | Neutral, slightly bright so the cut-out matches the image |
| Pacing | 1.1-1.3x, background switches on the sentence she starts talking about the next image |
| Sound | One soft whoosh on each background change; a "marker" or "pen" scribble under the red circle. Music off |

## Hook formulas
- "This [headline] is wrong. Here's why"
- "Someone asked me [comment]. Let's talk about it"
- "[Big company] just did [thing]. What it means for you"

## Presets to save in Edits
- Text: **Marker hook**, **Source chip**. Captions: **News caption**.

## Recipe
1. Cut-out + image background; key line zoomed big.
2. Hook 0-3 s (skip if the image itself is the hook). Source chip on every image.
3. Red circle on the key line when she reads it.

## Do / don't
- Do check the cut-out edge (no flicker around hair) and that the key line is readable on a phone.
- Don't use an image without a real source. Don't put captions over the key line.
